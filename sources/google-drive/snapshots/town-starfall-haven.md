﻿Starfall Haven
________________
1. Short Description
Starfall Haven is a discreet shanty-settlement hidden in an arroyo a few miles southeast of Roswell’s ruins. Half scavenger outpost, half covert alien enclave, the town grew around the wreckage of the CFS-Aurora, a small exploratory saucer that crashed decades ago. Locals who stumbled upon the site now coexist with the surviving extraterrestrials (slim, bioluminescent beings called the Lythari). Outsiders are tolerated—so long as they respect the town’s strict secrecy and “no-questions” policy.

4. Current Events & Quests
Event / Quest Hook
	Details
	Suspicious Signals
	Echo Spire picks up coded transmissions; party asked to trace or jam them before they reveal Haven’s location.
	Glow Blight Outbreak
	A fungus threatens the hydroponic gardens; players must gather rare desert reagents or alien enzymes to cure the crops.
	Broken Star Drive
	Zyrix believes a saucer component in Roswell Museum could restore limited flight—escort mission into hostile ruins.
	Raider Interest
	Local scavenger gang got wind of “glowing loot”; fortify stilt-towers or negotiate a false trail to divert them.
	Cultural Exchange Night
	Cantina hosts a music & story swap; PCs can earn trust (or secrets) through performance, trade, or diplomacy mini-games.
	Starfall Haven balances fragile cooperation between crash-landed aliens and wasteland survivors—offering the party intrigue, unique tech, and plenty of ways to shape this hidden town’s fate.
