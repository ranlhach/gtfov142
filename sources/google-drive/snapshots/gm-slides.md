West Marches - Basics
Whiteboard
Post apocalyptic / semi-barren or desert world
Before the apocalypse the technology was about where we are today
Magic and monsters were uncommon before the apocalypse, they are more common now
Will start with an unpopulated regional map with 2 starting locations
One vault
One village/town (think like an old west town)

Track any special political events for a given location
Add locales over time (to map, location, timeline)
Generally the world is fairly barren and while there can be vehicles, there is a lack of energy to power them (i.e. limited gas, limited fuel cells, etc)
Places can be of different magic/tech levels, but generally aim is for low magic/low tech
Generally limit STRONG weapons, it’s ok for them to exist once in a while, but a party shouldn’t be able to stockpile them.

Post apocalyptic world - cross between:
Mad max / Mad Mrox
Fallout
Terminator
Maybe Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind
History:
There had been times of magic and monsters long ago, but technology had taken over.
The world now:
About 100 years (4-5 generations) after the bombs fell
There air above is generally safe to breathe, but there are places that are unsafe to journey through and there are occasional storms.
There are pockets of civilization, villages, towns, a few cities, very few states and no nations. 
There are very few vehicles, the technologies to produce the energy needed for vehicles haven’t been restored.  The few vehicles that exist are typically battery driven (or fuel cell driven).  
Magic and Monsters:  with limited civilization both have risen from the ashes

Abandoned house
You are escorted to an airlock.  You are given a backpack with 1 week of water, 2 weeks of rations, a blanket, a matchbook(10), and if your character didn’t have a weapon a club.  If you generated a character you were also able to sneak out any starting gear the character had.
The door to the airlock closes behind you and the exterior door opens.  If the party does not exit then the airlock will start to charge with electricity.  Each minute, the airlock will do 1d6 damage to anyone in it until they exit.

Up a flight of stairs, you arrive in a rock strewn clearing. Around you are grey mountains.  The air smells dry but otherwise fine.  Roll random weather (not too brutal for first day).  There is what appears to be a deteriorating road heading to the northeast.  It is strewn with rocks and dirt but seems like it would be easily passable.  There are a few rabbits hopping around.

What does party do?

If party heads down road, they will reach a fork in the road.  If they head towards arabella, roll on random table.  Otherwise have them run into a party of raiders along the other road.

Smokey Bear
Smokey Bear lives in the mountains between Capitan and Arabella.  He can sense fires within the entire mountain range and will always arrive within 1d10 minutes of someone using fire of any kind.  
Smokey’s goals are primarily to put out any fires and will not be otherwise aggressive.  He will defend himself if attacked.
He either uses his shovel or his small rain cloud to put out the fire.
