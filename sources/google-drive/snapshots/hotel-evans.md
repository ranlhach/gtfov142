﻿Hotel Cauliflora


Residents:


* Milton
* Protecobots (2)
* Evans Family (6)




What’s going on:


Evans family
* Have taken up residence on the 2nd floor, they have taken over a suite of rooms (3 bedrooms total)
* They have discovered that the building where the carnivorous plant was is extremely fertile and have already been able to turn seeds into seedlings and even have some food growing.  
* They are attempting to covert some of the areas outside of that building into actual farm land. They are irrigating it with the water from the hotel and are having success so far by transpltanting some of the plants from the building.  They cannot explain why that building is so fertile but it’s like nothing they’ve ever seen. 
* (Reality:  there are some chemical barrels buried under that building that are causing the building to be extremely fertile.)
* Rabbit hutch:    the family has had luck with the rabbits and the training seems to be going ok, there haven’t been any mishaps and one of them is preggers.


Besides the farming:
* The Evans family has been extremely happy so far.  
* They would like to have more defenses.  
* They think in a few months they might even have food to support more people with the crops
* They would like the party to do something about the radioactive barrels in the pool, they don’t seem safe. 
* The Evans boy has has luck with the rabbits and so far they seem to be training well. 
   * One of the rabbits is even pregnant
* Family Argument: The stress of farming and adapting to a new life leads to a major argument between Jonah and his father, Eli, over how to handle their responsibilities. Jonah threatens to leave, creating tension in the family.
* Mysterious Illness: One of the younger children, Levi, falls ill, possibly from tainted water or a disease spread by desert wildlife. The family must find help or rare medicine to save him.
* Strange Visitor: A stranger arrives at the hotel claiming to know the Evans family from "before," but the family has no recollection of them. The visitor’s behavior is suspicious, causing distrust within the community.
