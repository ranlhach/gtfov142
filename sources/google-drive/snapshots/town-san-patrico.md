﻿Town Name: San Patrico
Description: San Patrico is a secluded town hidden in the New Mexico desert, mostly populated by ghouls who have lived since before the bombs fell. The town is known for its tight-knit community and wariness towards outsiders. Any visitors are required to hand over their weapons at the gate before being granted entry, ensuring the town's safety and control. Despite their rugged exterior, the ghouls are generally friendly and welcoming to those who prove trustworthy. Each resident of San Patrico retains skills from before the war, using their pre-apocalyptic expertise to keep their town thriving in the post-apocalyptic wasteland.

	The main social space for the ghouls, featuring homemade booze, old-world music on a salvaged jukebox, and a few well-used tables.

	A small tailor shop run by an ex-fashion designer ghoul. It has pre-war sewing machines, fabric, and threads for mending clothes.

	An improvised medical space with first-aid supplies and equipment. Run by an ex-doctor who provides care to anyone in need.

	A tinkering space where a former electrical engineer works on salvaging old-world technology, repairing radios and other electronics.
