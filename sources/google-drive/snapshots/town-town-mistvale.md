﻿Town Description
Mistvale is a secluded town nestled in the foothills of a mist-shrouded mountain range. The persistent fog that envelopes the area gives the town an ethereal and slightly mysterious ambiance. Known for its skilled artisans and silversmiths, Mistvale thrives on the trade of intricately crafted jewelry and decorative items. The town is also surrounded by dense forests, which provide both resources and a natural barrier against the outside world, making it a self-sufficient and close-knit community.
