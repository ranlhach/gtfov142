﻿Town Name: Capitan
Town Description:
Capitan is a small, post-apocalyptic town with a population that holds onto a deep reverence for the forested areas surrounding them. Nestled between lush woods that miraculously survived the devastation of the war, the people of Capitan believe they were spared thanks to Smokey the Bear, the mythical guardian of the forest. It is said that Smokey still roams the woods, extinguishing flames and keeping the land safe. Because of this belief, the people treat the forest as sacred ground, taking great care to ensure no harm comes to it. Their lives revolve around respecting nature, and they live in harmony with the thriving wildlife that flourishes under Smokey's protection.
