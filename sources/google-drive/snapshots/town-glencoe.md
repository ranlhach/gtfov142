﻿Town Name: Glencoe
Description:
Glencoe is a small but bustling post-apocalyptic town located in a relatively isolated area, making it the ideal hub for salvage and trade. The people of Glencoe are experts in scavenging and repurposing old-world technology, known for their ability to source parts for equipment that others consider impossible to find. They welcome traders and travelers with open arms, offering a variety of items salvaged from the wasteland in exchange for goods or information. However, Glencoe harbors a secret: one of its buildings is filled with children tasked with cranking hand generators to create electricity, ensuring the town has enough power for its essential needs. This secret is fiercely protected, and only a handful of trusted residents know the truth.
