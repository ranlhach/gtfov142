Original Details
* Arabella is a small, resilient post-apocalyptic town with a population of about 100 people. Nestled in a once-thriving region, it now stands as a beacon of survival and hope amidst the ruins of a fallen civilization. The town is characterized by its resourcefulness and close-knit community, where every resident plays a vital role in ensuring the collective well-being.
* Geography and Environment:
* Arabella is situated in a relatively fertile area, which allows for some agriculture and access to water sources like a nearby river or lake. The town is surrounded by remnants of the old world, such as decaying buildings, overgrown roads, and abandoned vehicles, which the residents repurpose for their needs.
* Architecture and Infrastructure:
* The buildings in Arabella are a mix of old and new. Many structures have been patched up using salvaged materials, giving the town a patchwork appearance. Common areas include a communal gathering space, makeshift homes, and a central storage area for supplies. Solar panels and wind turbines are scattered around to provide renewable energy.
* Economy and Resources:
* Arabella's economy is based on barter and trade, both within the town and with neighboring settlements. The residents grow crops, raise livestock, hunt, and forage to secure their food supply. Water is sourced from wells or purified from natural sources. Workshops and craft areas are dedicated to repairing tools, clothing, and equipment.
* Governance and Security:
* The town is governed by Mayor Elias Stone, a strong and wise leader who has earned the trust of the community. He oversees a small council that helps make decisions and enforce rules. Security is a priority, with a volunteer militia organized to protect the town from external threats, including hostile groups and wild animals.
* Social Structure and Community:
* The community of Arabella is tight-knit, with strong social bonds and a sense of shared purpose. Residents support one another through labor, skills exchange, and emotional support. Regular meetings and communal activities foster a sense of unity and cooperation.
* Challenges and Adaptation:
* Life in Arabella is not without its challenges. The town must constantly adapt to changing environmental conditions, potential resource shortages, and external threats. However, the resilience, ingenuity, and determination of its people ensure that Arabella continues to thrive in the face of adversity.
* Culture and Identity:
* Despite the harsh conditions, the people of Arabella have cultivated a unique culture that celebrates their survival and community spirit. Festivals, storytelling, and music are integral parts of their lives, providing moments of joy and preserving their heritage.
* Arabella stands as a testament to human perseverance, where the past and present merge to create a hopeful future.

Lilah - Scavenger & Farmer


Lilah lost a necklace while exploring a nuclear research facility east of Tinnie.  There were various drones and robots in there that she was able to sneak past at the time until she ran into one protectron.  She tried to fight the protectron but eventually had to retreat.  She had a necklace but it got snagged on the protectron in her escape.  She is hoping that either the protectron still has it or it is in the facility somewhere.  The necklace is very valuable to her and if the party can get it back she would be grateful.  She’d be willing to pay 100 caps + a Tesla Rapier.
