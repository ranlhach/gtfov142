﻿Brotherhood of Wood Forward Base (Roswell Airport)
________________


1. Short Public Description
Hidden beneath the cracked tarmac of Roswell Municipal Airport lies the Brotherhood of Wood Forward Base, an underground stronghold run by a techno-druidic chapter whose creed matches the Brotherhood of Steel—only their reverence is for pre-war forestry tech, bio-engineering, and renewable power. From camouflaged hangars they venture into the wastes to recover lost machines, protect verdant relics, and stamp out “tech abuse.” Surface signs are minimal: an overgrown runway and a single rust-eaten control tower used as a lookout.

4. Current Events & Quest Hooks
Event / Quest
	GM Details
	Rotor Ransom
	Some Mutants  stole a vertibird rotor assembly; Knights seek volunteers to retrieve it before the vertibird project stalls.
	Blight in the Nursery
	Fungal infection threatens rare seedlings; party must gather anti-fungal compounds from irradiated mangroves south of Roswell.
	Runway Ghost Signal
	Mysterious SOS pings from an old control beacon; investigating could reveal a hidden vault—or trap.
	Timber Treaty
	Nearby settlement wants lumber tech aid; Paladin Cedar asks PCs to broker terms without revealing the base’s location.
	Wasteland Arbor Day
	Annual ceremony topside: planting saplings in crater soil while fending off mutant locusts—players can defend or lead festivities.
	Star-hidden beneath Roswell’s runways, the Brotherhood of Wood blends militant reclamation with eco-mysticism—ripe for alliance, intrigue, or conflict in your wasteland campaign.
