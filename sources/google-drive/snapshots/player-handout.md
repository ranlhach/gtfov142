Some High level Basics
Rule set:
Mork Borg / Mad Morx rules (Mork Borg + Cars / Guns)
Setting:
Post apocalyptic, something between Mad Max, Fallout, Terminator, etc.
Before the apocalypse the tech similar to what we have now
Magic and monsters were uncommon before the bombs fell
Adventures / Sandbox
Have settled on a high level map to start with
But except for a few locations/starter adventures plan to generate adventures and the world on the fly  (similar to a West Marches style campaign)
Generally plan to be able to complete adventures in 1-2 sessions of 2-3 hours each.
Content Generation
I’m trying to use AI (ChatGPT + Dall-E) to generate maps and some of the filler content
Character Generation / Death
Can start as a normal person (use adventure) or a MB scvmbirther 
Starting equipment will be meager.  You expect to be entering a wasteland so you’ll need to scavenge.
After the initial session if characters die, they will be replaced with denizens from the world outside the vault. 
Characters knocked to 0 or below will enter the dying state.  This applies to most monsters as well.  

Initial 
You were born in a underground habitat, often called a vault.  You believe that you and everyone else surviving may be all that remains of humanity and that you are kept in the vault to live and breed until the world is safe.
Your day to day lives are spent maintaining underground farms during the day and entertainment and drugs at night.  There is a library of thousands of videos and video games.  
The general population is kept docile through the use of a pain collar and the threat of exile.  Anyone who breaks any of the serious laws are exiled.  Additionally, the General Population is kept to a maximum of 100 people in your group.  Anytime that there is more than 100, then the sick, the old, or the too mutated are are exiled.
Oh yes, and about one out of every 4 births result in some sort of mutation.  Some mutants have special powers, some can use magic, and many are just weird.
You have been exiled and that’s where we will begin.

The Basics
Your character has 4 abilities, that can range from -3 (the worst)  to +6 (the best):
STRENGTH - Crush, lift, strike, grapple
AGILITY - Defend, balance, swim, flee
PRESENCE - Perceive, aim, charm, wield Powers
TOUGHNESS - Resist poison/cold/heat, survive falling

Many challenges and tests are resolved by role-playing; others can be settled by rolling a d20 die. To see if you succeed at something challenging (i.e., "perform a test"), roll a d20 and add the relevant ability score. If you roll sufficiently high--equal to or greater than the target "Difficulty Rating" (DR), as decided by the GM--you succeed.

Your character has 2 other important stats:
HIT POINTS - When you get hit by weapons, spells, monsters, etc you take damage and reduce your hit points. When you run out of hit points, you become "broken"... which is bad. You regain hit points by resting or using healing items.
Broken: roll a d4… maybe you'll fall unconscious, maybe you'll lose a limb, or maybe you'll just immediately die. Exciting!
Resting: regain d4 hit points for a short rest (take a drink of water), d6 after a full night's rest.
Starving: lose d4 hit points per day (can't rest).
Infected: lose d6 hit points per day (can't rest).
OMENS - think of these as "luck points." You have a very small number of them, and by spending an omen, you can change the outcome of one die roll. E.g., deal max damage with an attack, reroll a dice roll (yours or someone else's), lower damage dealt to you by d6, neutralize a critical (20) or fumble (1) roll, or lower one test DR by 4. A well-spent omen can definitely save your ass. Regain omens by resting at least 6 hours only after you use them all.

Basic Vehicle Rules
Stats:
HP:  amount of damage car can take
Str: affects ramming ability (ie {str} x d6)
Agi:  nimbleness - avoid being shot, turning ability / acceleration (min 1)
Pre:  max speed (Pre x 20MPH)
Tou:  damage reduction - to vehicle and/or occupants (1d {Toughness})
Combat
Movement
Maneuvering - con do the 
Turn up to 45’
Accelerate or decelerate 10MPH
Car moves speed forward (or backward) after maneuvering
Car v Car
Cars that are within 25’ of a car or person during a round can do following 
Drive makes an agility check (modified by the opponent vehicle agi)
Sideswipe:   {str} x d4 (if from side)
Ram {str} x d6 (if from rear or t-bone) + MPH/10
Damage above 10 also affects the ramming car
Damage from speed is relative
People v People
People in a car gain 1d{Toughness} armor reduction
People in a car gain Agility to DR to hit
Travel
Uses one fuel cell per 250 miles
