﻿Dust Raiders
Dust Raiders are a group of individuals who once thrived as ruthless raiders but have since evolved into a more principled band, adopting a "Robin Hood" approach to survival. They operate near Ruidoso Downs, a place once known for its horse racing and now a semi-ruined desert settlement where the Dust Raiders have established their base of operations.
Dust Raiders Characteristics:
* Background: Originally, the Dust Raiders were typical raiders—violent, opportunistic, and driven by the desire for resources in a post-apocalyptic wasteland. However, over time, they became disillusioned with mindless pillaging and began to see the potential for building something better. They decided to use their skills to protect and provide for those in need while still living by their own rules.
* Moral Code: They follow a strict, self-imposed code of ethics. They still rob travelers and nearby settlements, but they only take from the wealthy or exploitative traders. The Dust Raiders redistribute their loot to struggling communities and downtrodden families. They have become known as both bandits and protectors—feared by the rich, but respected by the desperate.
* Appearance: The Dust Raiders are a motley group, wearing mismatched armor, salvaged protective gear, and clothing adapted to the desert environment. They use scarves and makeshift masks to shield their faces from the relentless sand and dust storms. They often adorn their attire with symbols of rebellion—painted insignias, animal bones, or pieces of old flags.
* Base of Operations: Their base, which they call “The Downs Hideout”, is built amidst the ruins of the old Ruidoso Downs racetrack. They have fortified the remaining grandstand structures and converted stables into living quarters. Underground maintenance tunnels serve as their storerooms and armories, while the old betting booths have become watchtowers where guards keep a lookout for threats.
* Tactics: The Dust Raiders still rely on guerrilla tactics that they perfected as raiders—ambushing supply caravans, using hit-and-run attacks, and disappearing back into the desert before any reinforcements arrive. They are known to use non-lethal traps and diversions to minimize unnecessary bloodshed.
* Leadership: The Dust Raiders are led by Inez “Dust Shadow” Martinez, a charismatic and cunning leader who believes in the ideals of fair distribution and mutual survival. She is respected for her keen strategic mind and her ability to read people’s motives, making her an effective judge of which targets deserve to be robbed and which people need protection.
* Relationship with Locals: The locals in Ruidoso Downs and the surrounding area have mixed feelings about the Dust Raiders. Many appreciate the help they have received, whether it be food, water, or protection, while others remain wary of their criminal past. The Dust Raiders maintain a delicate balance between outlaw and savior.
* Weapons and Resources: Their arsenal is composed of salvaged weapons—rifles, crossbows, melee weapons pieced together from scrap, and even a few pre-war firearms. Many of their vehicles are cobbled-together dune buggies and motorcycles, perfect for navigating the rugged terrain. They rely heavily on speed and adaptability, preferring mobility over brute strength.
The Dust Raiders represent a group seeking redemption. Though they still operate outside the boundaries of the law, they have chosen to use their skills for what they believe is a higher purpose, helping those who are too often overlooked in the unforgiving wastelands.

Name: Thad "Dusthand" Rooker
Age: 34
Occupation: Former Dust Raider, current resident of Arabella
Description:
Thad is a rugged-looking man in his mid-thirties, with dark hair tied back in a short ponytail and a thick beard speckled with sand and dust. His skin is tanned and weathered, a testament to years spent traveling through the unforgiving desert as a member of the Dust Raiders. Thad is often seen wearing desert-friendly attire—sturdy boots, cargo pants, and a well-worn leather jacket adorned with insignias from his past, including the Dust Raiders' emblem. His eyes carry a sharp, calculating gaze, always assessing the people and situations around him.
Personality:
Thad has a strong sense of loyalty and justice—qualities that drew him to the Dust Raiders' more noble cause when they shifted from traditional raiding to a "Robin Hood" style of banditry. Though he now lives in Arabella, married to one of the town's merchants, his heart still beats for the Dust Raiders' mission. He is passionate about helping the less fortunate and despises those who exploit others for profit. Thad can be charming and persuasive, able to talk his way out of trouble or gather support when needed. He is also fiercely protective of his loved ones and the community of Arabella, and he will not hesitate to take action if he perceives a threat.
Role in Arabella:
Since settling down in Arabella, Thad has become part of the community, assisting his spouse with their merchant business. He helps maintain their shop and is a familiar face at the town’s market, often seen handling supplies or bartering for goods. However, Thad's allegiance to the Dust Raiders remains strong, and he occasionally slips away to aid his former comrades. He acts as their eyes and ears in Arabella, providing them with information and occasionally smuggling supplies to those in need.
Relationship with Locals:
Thad is well-liked in Arabella. His charm and willingness to lend a hand have earned him the respect of many in the community. Though some may be suspicious of his past, his genuine love for his merchant spouse and his contributions to Arabella's safety have gone a long way in solidifying his place in the town. The children in Arabella are especially fond of Thad, as he often tells stories of his daring adventures on the road—albeit toned down for young ears.
Recruitment Efforts:
Lately, Thad has been seen in the shadows of the marketplace and the local tavern, whispering to those he deems capable and trustworthy. He is recruiting for a group to deal with a rising problem—a rival gang known as The Scorpions, who have been harassing traders and trying to push into Arabella’s territory. Thad knows that the Dust Raiders can't handle this threat alone without risking their position, so he has taken it upon himself to find people willing to join him in confronting these dangerous rivals. He is careful with whom he approaches, preferring those with a sense of justice or those who have reasons to despise The Scorpions.
Special Skills:
* Combat Experience: Thad is highly skilled in hand-to-hand combat and is well-versed in using scavenged weapons, particularly melee ones. He prefers using a rugged axe, which he keeps sharpened and ready for action.
* Tactician: Thad is adept at reading situations, planning ambushes, and adapting quickly to changing circumstances—skills honed during his time with the Dust Raiders.
* Charismatic Recruiter: Thad has a natural ability to connect with people, understanding what motivates them. His recruitment style is less about force and more about rallying people to a worthy cause.
Current Goals:
* Protect Arabella: Thad wants to ensure that Arabella remains safe from outside threats, particularly from exploitative gangs like The Scorpions. He is willing to put himself on the line to protect the town and the people he cares about.
* Support the Dust Raiders: While Thad’s life has changed since marrying and settling in Arabella, he still believes in the Dust Raiders’ mission. He wants to help them grow stronger and maintain their Robin Hood-like operations.
* Build a Strong Team: Thad is focused on recruiting capable individuals for a mission to deal with The Scorpions—either by confronting them directly or sabotaging their operations to keep them away from Arabella.
Notable Relationships:
* Spouse: Thad is deeply in love with his spouse, Lena, a skilled merchant who has a knack for acquiring rare goods. Lena is the main reason Thad decided to settle down, and she is his biggest anchor to Arabella. Despite her disapproval of his dangerous lifestyle, Lena understands Thad's desire to protect those who cannot protect themselves.
* The Dust Raiders: Thad is still on good terms with the Dust Raiders and keeps in contact whenever possible. They trust Thad to be their informant and ally within Arabella. He uses his connections to keep them well-informed about the town's happenings and the surrounding threats.
Thad is a man caught between two worlds—his past as a Dust Raider and his present as a resident of Arabella. But to him, these two lives are not in conflict; they are both driven by his desire to protect and uplift those who need it most.
