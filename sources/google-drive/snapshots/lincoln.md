﻿Arrival
This is the town of Lincoln, or what remains of it.  There are several semi-intact buildings and what looks to be a mostly intact robot factory.  There is occasional movement, but you would need to get closer to investigate.
Main building - Robot Factory


Mr Rivet


Aspect
	Description
	Name
	Mr. Rivet - The robot maintaining the factory. His brain is that of the original owner, Howard Riverton, implanted into an old Mr. Handy model to extend his life.
	Wants
	He is lonely.  He is looking to make friend
	Appearance
	- Type: An old Mr. Handy model modified for heavy-duty tasks, with three extended arms (welder, claw hand, outdated laser torch).
- Features: Rust patches, flickering visual sensors, engraved initials (H.R.).
- Modifications: Reinforced compartment for his biological brain, with occasional steam or coolant puffs.
	Mannerisms
	- Constant Chatter: Loves to talk, often recounting stories of the "good old days" mixed with mechanical jargon and tangents.
- Formal and Polite: Uses formal language like sir, ma’am, and insists on giving a "factory tour."
	Obsessive Maintenance
	Fixated on maintaining the factory "up to code," often seen trying to fix broken machinery, even when it’s beyond saving.
	Mood Swings
	Experiences mood swings due to the fusion of organic and mechanical parts. Shifts between optimism about "rebuilding the world" and despair at the factory’s state.
	Proud of His Work
	Proud of the robots he produced before the war, refers to the inactive bots as "my children" and tries to maintain them as best as possible.
	Quirky Habits
	- Singing: Often sings off-key factory work songs while working.
- Third-Person Reference: Refers to himself as Mr. Rivet when talking about himself.
	Special Quirks
	- Occasional Malfunctions: The brain-computer interface glitches, causing confusion or attempts to perform pre-war routines like taking attendance or writing reports.
- Unyielding Loyalty: Believes he still owns the factory and is wary of intruders, especially if they threaten to dismantle the factory or its bots.
	

Inside


Aspect
	Description
	Building Exterior
	The factory is a large, rectangular structure built from reinforced concrete. The outer walls are cracked and covered with vines, showing signs of age and damage. Broken windows are scattered throughout, and parts of the roof have collapsed in some areas. Despite the damage, the building still stands largely intact, giving an impression of resilience. A faded sign reading "Lincoln Robotics Corp" hangs loosely above the main entrance.
	Building 
Interior
	Inside, the factory is a mix of chaos and disrepair. The reception area has broken furniture and dusty flooring, with a few exposed wires hanging from the ceiling. The hallways are dimly lit, with flickering lights casting eerie shadows. Some areas are blocked by fallen debris. The robot assembly areas are a mix of metal tables, conveyor belts, and scattered machinery. Broken parts and old robot components are piled up in the corners, while some half-constructed robots lie forgotten.
	Rooms
	

	Reception Area
	The reception area has a broken front desk covered in dust, some faded posters of pre-war advertisements, and an old terminal that no longer works.
	Office 1
	The first office is filled with broken desks and chairs. Papers are strewn all over, including some design schematics for early robot models.
	Office 2
	The second office has an overturned filing cabinet, a rusted safe (locked), and a wall-mounted map showing emergency evacuation routes.
	Cafeteria
	The cafeteria has long tables, overturned chairs, and broken vending machines. There are old, moldy food packets on some tables, and a water dispenser (empty).
	Robot Assembly Room 1
	The first assembly room has metal arms and conveyor belts. Some incomplete robot frames hang from hooks, and there are containers of bolts, wires, and other small parts.
	Robot Assembly Room 2
	The second assembly room has a large central table with multiple robotic limbs scattered across it. A few robotic heads are on shelves, some with cracked visors and disconnected wiring.
	Items Found Inside
	

	- Bots
	- A few inactive Protectrons and two Mister Handy bots, all in various states of disrepair.
	- Turrets
	- Wall-mounted turrets near the entrances and in the hallways. Some are operational but lack power, while others are completely dismantled.
	- Robot Parts
	- Robotic limbs, chassis, control modules, and various small components scattered throughout the assembly rooms and storage areas.
	- Tools
	- Wrenches, welding torches, and toolboxes, most of which are rusty but still usable.
	- Power Cores
	- Two intact fusion power cores found in a locked storage compartment.
	- Scrap Metal
	- Scrap metal piles, including gears, bolts, and broken conveyor belt sections, are found in most corners of the assembly areas.
	- Blueprints and Schematics
	- Design schematics for different robot models, including modifications for defense and specialized tasks. Some are torn or faded, but others are legible.
	- Terminals
	- Several old terminals throughout the building, most of which are non-functional, but one in the main office still has power and displays old production data.
	- Old Security Bot
	- An inactive security bot with its legs missing, but the upper body and weapons intact, found in the second assembly room.

The Bots




  
  
  
Lincoln appears as a desolate whisper of its former self, nestled among the sparse desert scrub. The small town's silhouette is dominated by the skeletal remains of its buildings, each etched against the harsh skyline in varying degrees of decay. The once vibrant town hall and the crumbling schoolhouse tell a story of abrupt abandonment, while the partially collapsed roofs of the general store and mechanic's garage hint at years of neglect. A faint glimmer from broken stained glass pieces of the church catches the light at times, providing a stark contrast to the overall gloom. The eerie stillness is occasionally disrupted by the mechanical whirr and clank of patrolling robots, remnants of a forgotten security system now gone rogue, adding a layer of foreboding to the desolate scene.
