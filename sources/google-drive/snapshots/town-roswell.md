﻿Step 1: Town Name
* Name: Roswell
Step 2: Town Description
Roswell, set in the year 2200, has evolved from its storied past into a hub of both human and secret alien activity. The town retains its fame as the alleged site of a UFO crash in 1947, which has profoundly influenced its culture and architecture. With buildings and streets that reflect a blend of futuristic and retro-UFO styles, Roswell serves as a tourist destination and a covert meeting ground for interstellar diplomacy. While most inhabitants go about their daily lives, some are aliens in disguise, quietly steering the town’s development and guarding their technology.
