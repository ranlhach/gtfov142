﻿* Experiment:  
   * Vault 142’s experiments were to expose people to a rock that seemed to had an unexplainable energy coming from it.  The energy seemed to mutate some people.
* The Vault dynamics
   * Vault layout
      * 7 living sections (bedrooms, community centers, education, entertainment, exercise)
      * Water / Energy section:  there are two sections that handle water and energy
      * There are 5 farming sections, each grows enough food for approximately 1000 people
         * Excess is stored
      * Warehouses
         * There are vast caves of food storage and other goods.  Only the Elite guard has access to it (and the Overseer)
   * Overseer / Ruling class:
      * Overseer (Leader + Family - 4 )
      * Leaders (5 + families - 20)
      * Engineers (10+10 + children - 40)
      * Elite Guard (and their families 20+20+children - 75-100 )
   * GP (General Population aka Guinea Pigs - 500 total)
      * Kept in 5 groups of maximum 100
      * The GP does all the menial work and are only educated to ~middle school level
   * Politics and keeping the peace
      * Overseer is the supreme leader of the vault, 
      * Each leader commands a section
      * Elite guard is the only group with weapons
      * GP is kept unarmed and docile through entertainment, recreational drugs, and pain band necklaces.  People deemed uncontrollable are exiled to the wastelands where they are sent to die.
      * Population is kept to a maximum of 700 people.  If the GP goes over 700, then people are chosen for exile:  
         * Elderly, Sick, deplorables (those with powers that the elite guard doesn’t want to deal with)
