## Roadblock Skirmish Description: The party encounters a makeshift barricade set up by mutan

Roadblock Skirmish 
Description: The party encounters a makeshift barricade set up by mutant warriors. They demand tolls or challenge travelers to combat.   
Outcome: The party can negotiate safe passage, try to force their way through, or find a way around if they scout the area effectively.   
Ambush Site  
Description: A section of the road dips between two rocky outcroppings. Mutant warriors use the high ground to ambush unsuspecting travelers.   
Outcome: If the party detects them early, they can set up defenses or launch a preemptive strike. Otherwise, they get caught in a crossfire.    
Rescued Captive 
Description: The party stumbles upon a lone traveler held captive by mutants. The traveler is being forced to carry supplies or perform menial tasks.  
Outcome: Freeing the captive could lead to rewards, or the captive might have critical information about the mutants’ camp or the area.    
Strange Barter 
Description: The mutants, surprisingly, approach the party not with hostility but with an offer to trade. They hold exotic items, possibly pre-war technology or curiosities.  
Outcome: Negotiations could lead to peaceful exchanges, or the mutants might be trying to trick the party into lowering their guard.    
Overheard Ritual 
Description: At dusk, chanting and drums echo through the desert. Investigating reveals a mutant shaman leading a ritual for strength or healing.  
Outcome: Observing might reveal insights into mutant culture or potential alliances. However, interrupting the ritual could provoke aggression.    
Cursed Oasis  
Description: A rare water source in the desert, rumored to be cursed. Mutant warriors guard it jealously, claiming it belongs to their tribe.  
Outcome: If the party tries to take water without permission, the mutants respond with violence. Diplomatic negotiation might earn them access to the oasis.    
Abandoned Checkpoint 
Description: An old military checkpoint or toll booth, partially collapsed. Mutants occasionally squat there, using the structure for ambushes or shade.  
Outcome: The party could take advantage of leftover supplies or salvage, but must contend with mutants who consider it part of their territory.    
Migrating Herd   
Description: A herd of large, mutated herbivores crosses the road, accompanied by several mutant warriors who protect them in exchange for a steady food source.  
Outcome: The party either waits for the herd to pass or navigates around it, potentially interacting with the mutants in a less hostile context—though any perceived threat to the herd triggers aggression.
