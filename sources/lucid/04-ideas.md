😂 Funny Encounters 
 
The Ultimate Salesman – A persistent ghoul wearing a tattered suit tries to sell the party “genuine pre-war insurance” that “totally still works.” 
Mutant Chicken Chase – A massive, two-headed chicken escapes from a traveling merchant, causing chaos as everyone tries to catch it. 
Roadside Clown – A lone, tired-looking man in full clown makeup sits on a crate, eating beans and muttering, “Used to be a party city rep... now look at me.”  
😨 Weird Encounters 
 
Reverse Echo – The party hears their own voices echoing back, but saying things they haven’t said yet. 
The Laughing Billboard – An old, crumbling electronic billboard flickers to life, playing a looping laugh track despite no visible power source. 
The Desert Walker – A tall, skeletal figure wrapped in bandages trudges across the dunes. It never acknowledges the party and never stops walking.  
☠️ Dangerous Encounters 
 
Boomer Corpses – A field of dead raiders is rigged with motion-sensitive explosives—get too close, and they go off. 
Sand Trap – The road suddenly collapses, revealing a massive underground nest of mutated centipedes. 
Sniper’s Game – A voice crackles over an unseen speaker: “Welcome to the gauntlet. Run fast, or run dead.” Shots ring out.
