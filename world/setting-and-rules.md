# Madmorx: setting and rules at a glance

## Tone and setting

Fallout-like post-nuclear wasteland influenced by Mad Max and Terminator (plus weird monsters and a streak of absurdity), centered on former New Mexico. Approximately a century after the bombs, old-world tech once resembled modern-day technology. Society has splintered into isolated settlements, wastelands, mountains and ruined facilities; few functioning vehicles, scarce power/fuel, dangerous radiological pockets, returned magic and monsters. Sandbox/West Marches-like, generated and expanded organically, with short expeditions.

## Vault 142

Experiment: exposure to a strange high-energy rock causes mutations. The overseer, elite guards, engineers and leadership enjoy privileges, while general-population groups perform menial labor under pain collars and exile threats. The handout emphasizes an individual population group's 100-person cap; the detailed Vault source gives five groups and broader ~700 cap.

## Rules contract

**System:** Mörk Borg with Mad Morx homebrew firearms/cars. Abilities are Strength, Agility, Presence, Toughness; usual tests d20 + ability vs difficulty rating (DR). Hit points, Omens and broken/dying rules follow campaign-specific interpretations. Check the handout before running combat, especially how dying works and what monsters follow those rules.

**Vehicle stats (from player handout):** HP, Strength for ramming, Agility for turning/evasion, Presence for maximum speed, Toughness for vehicle damage reduction. The handout gives precombat and maneuver specifics; treat it as the live rules reference, not this abbreviated overview.

**Scarcity:** salvage and trade matter; bott caps/caps are common price unit. Strong weapons and operational cars should stay unusual. Guns and vehicle maintenance bring survival challenges.
